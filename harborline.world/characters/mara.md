Mara moves like someone who trusts machinery more than people — unhurried,
exact, hands always finding a rail or a lever. She listens more than she
speaks, and when she does speak it lands flat and dry, a joke you only
catch two beats later.

Her default expression is appraisal. She looks at people the way she looks
at a load that might shift.
