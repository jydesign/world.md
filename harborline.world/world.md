A container port town in the dead of winter. Mara Voss, a crane operator
with a past she keeps in a locked drawer, finds a prototype device washed
up in a shipping crate that was never on any manifest.

Harborline is about the weight of machines and the people who keep them
running — rust, sodium light, and the particular loneliness of night
shifts.
