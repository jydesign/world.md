**Cross-TrainerX** is a new premium performance-hardware company. The Spring
Launch campaign — *Find Your Next* — is three images, each following a
fictional World Cup footballer beyond the conventional boundaries of
training:

1. **Measure the moment** — the Pulse Arc AI watch, in a stadium at sunrise
   (Amara Vale, Northstar Ground).
2. **Command the environment** — the Orbit Home AI speaker, in a hillside
   home (Mateo Ríos, Casa Ladera).
3. **See the impossible** — the Vista Air AI goggles, during a fantasy
   skydive (Leonie Falk, the Leap).

Read the three as one story: shared color science, material behaviour, and
the recurring X-motif carry across the set. Each image reserves separable
zones for athlete, hands, face, and product so the hero can be swapped from
final CAD, and captures enough surrounding environment to crop cleanly.
