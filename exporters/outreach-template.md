# Early-user outreach template

The cover note that goes with the two files handed to each early user
(weeks 5–8 of PLAN.md). Send **README.md** + **exporters/spec-prompt.md**
as attachments; this note tells them the order and the ask.

The wording is doing quiet work for the experiment — read the notes at the
bottom before editing.

---

Subject: a rough thing I built — would you kick the tires?

Hi [name],

[One personal hook — e.g. "You've mentioned how much time you burn
re-describing the same characters to Midjourney. This is the thing I've
been building for exactly that."]

It's called WORLD.md: a plain-text way to write down a project's
characters, products, locations, and style once, so AI tools stop making
you re-explain them every prompt. It's early and rough, and honestly I'm
trying to find out whether it's useful to anyone but me.

Two files attached:
• README.md — read this first (~5 min). The what and why.
• spec-prompt.md — paste it into Claude or ChatGPT and it'll interview you
  and build your world with you. That's the fastest way in.

The ask: take one real project you're actively working on — not a test —
put it into the format, and use it for a week the way you normally
generate. Then tell me, bluntly, where it fell down: where you had to
re-explain something it should've known, what drifted anyway, where you
gave up. "I quit at step 2" is genuinely the most useful thing you can
send me.

Not public yet, so please keep it between us for now. No rush and no
polish expected — thank you for taking a look.

[James]

---

## Notes (do not send these)

- **Personalize the hook, keep the rest.** The first line earns the open;
  the body is deliberately un-salesy. This is alpha — honesty recruits
  better than hype, and the un-polish is the point.
- **It asks for ONE project, not "and then try another."** The gate is
  whether ≥2 people reach for it on a SECOND project *unprompted*.
  Mentioning a second project contaminates the exact signal being
  measured. Let it happen on its own.
- **When someone gets stuck, resist helping — record where they stuck.**
  Per PLAN: if they need you to explain it, that IS the finding. Every
  rescue erases a data point. Capture the friction verbatim into
  FEEDBACK.md.
- **Do not demo; observe.** One debrief conversation per user, notes
  verbatim. The debrief is observation, not support.
- **For Discord / community DMs**: drop the subject line; "keep it between
  us" softens to "not public yet." Otherwise send as-is.
