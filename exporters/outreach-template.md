# Early-user outreach templates

Two asks, in escalating order. **Start with A.** B is for anyone who bites,
or for someone you already know has the pain (a working concept artist,
an AI filmmaker). Sending B cold is why four people said "this is amazing"
and none of them tried it — see FEEDBACK 018.

Repo: https://github.com/jydesign/world.md

---

## A — the five-minute ask (default, first touch)

Subject: 5 minutes? I want to know if this makes sense to anyone but me

Hi [name],

[One personal hook — e.g. "You've mentioned how much time you burn
re-describing the same characters to Midjourney. This is the thing I've
been building for exactly that."]

I built a plain-text format for describing a creative world — characters,
products, locations, style — so AI tools stop making you re-explain it in
every prompt. It's called WORLD.md.

I'm not asking you to adopt anything. Five minutes:

1. Open the attached file (spec-prompt.md)
2. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude
3. Answer its questions about any project — real, half-formed, or invented
   on the spot
4. Send me whatever it builds

That's the whole thing. What I want to know is whether it makes sense to
anyone but me: where it confused you, what you expected it to ask and
didn't, whether the output looks like something you'd ever use.

Blunt beats polite. "I didn't get it" is the most useful thing you could
send me.

[James]

---

## B — the week-long ask (for anyone who bites)

Subject: a rough thing I built — would you kick the tires?

Hi [name],

[One personal hook.]

It's called WORLD.md: a plain-text way to write down a project's
characters, products, locations, and style once, so AI tools stop making
you re-explain them every prompt. It's early and rough, and honestly I'm
trying to find out whether it's useful to anyone but me.

Start here: https://github.com/jydesign/world.md — the README is a ~5 min
read. Then paste `exporters/spec-prompt.md` into Claude or ChatGPT and
it'll interview you and build your world with you.

The ask: take one real project you're actively working on — not a test —
put it into the format, and use it for a week the way you normally
generate. Then tell me, bluntly, where it fell down: where you had to
re-explain something it should've known, what drifted anyway, where you
gave up. "I quit at step 2" is genuinely the most useful thing you can
send me.

No rush and no polish expected — thank you for taking a look.

[James]

## Notes (do not send these)

- **Lead with A.** A five-minute ask with a numbered list converts; a
  week-long commitment reads as a favor and gets "this is amazing" instead
  of a yes. A also removes the hidden blocker in B — that they need a real
  project ready before they can start.
- **A produces an artifact.** Even if they never generate an image, they
  come back with a world the AI built, which is something concrete to react
  to and a natural opening for B.
- **Personalize the hook, keep the rest.** The first line earns the open;
  the body is deliberately un-salesy. This is alpha — honesty recruits
  better than hype, and the un-polish is the point.
- **B asks for ONE project, not "and then try another."** The gate is
  whether ≥2 people reach for it on a SECOND project *unprompted*.
  Mentioning a second project contaminates the exact signal being measured.
- **When someone gets stuck, resist helping — record where they stuck.**
  Per PLAN: if they need you to explain it, that IS the finding. Every
  rescue erases a data point. Capture the friction verbatim in FEEDBACK.md.
- **Do not demo; observe.** One debrief per user, notes verbatim.
- **For Discord / community DMs**: drop the subject line, paste the repo
  link instead of attaching, and use A.
